# How to Stay Consistent With Your Studies

> **This is an EXAMPLE article for the Zavia agent.** It is safe to publish
> to test the agent, but delete it from the admin panel afterwards.
> The tags above are brand-new example tags — do not reuse old tags,
> because the publish API fails (500) when a tag already exists.

Motivation comes and goes. Systems stay. Here are three small habits:

## 1. Study at the same time every day

Pick one fixed hour — for example, right after Fajr or after dinner —
and protect it. Your brain learns the rhythm within two weeks.

## 2. Make the first 5 minutes tiny

Open your notes and read one page. That is all. Starting is the hard
part; once you start, continuing is easy.

## 3. Track it on paper

Put a tick on a wall calendar for every day you study. Do not break the
chain. A missed day is fine — two missed days is the start of quitting.

## The bottom line

You do not need more motivation. You need a small, fixed routine that
you can repeat even on a bad day.
